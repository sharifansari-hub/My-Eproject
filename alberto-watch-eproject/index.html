Sapphire crystal

Sapphire is one of the hardest transparent materials, second only to diamond, so keys and desks rarely scratch it. Our luxury watches and the S2 Smart Classic use it. Mineral glass costs less and scratches more easily.

About us

A full-service watch repair, appraisal and retail store.

Alberto Watch Company offers an unparalleled selection of fine timepieces with first-class service. We carry new watches from Michael Kors, Rolex, Citizen Eco-Drive, Bulova and more, alongside restored vintage pieces, smart watches and clocks.

Every watch we sell can be serviced at our own workshop, and every watch we service is looked at by a watchmaker, not a machine. If you are not sure what your watch is worth, we will appraise it and give you the valuation in writing.

Our range is organised by line-up: Vintage, Luxury, Smart Watches, Sport & Casual, and Clocks. If you cannot find what you want, ask us. We can often source it.
